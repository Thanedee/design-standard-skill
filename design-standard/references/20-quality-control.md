# 20. Quality control: scoring and pre-flight checklists

A design is approved only when it has no critical failure and scores at least 8/10 in every category; a high average never rescues a failure in accuracy, legibility, hierarchy, accessibility, rights or brand. Run the relevant checklist on the exported file.

### 20.1 Critical failures (any one = revise, whatever the score)

1. Any wrong word, number, name, date, price or contact detail.
2. Any invented fact, logo, seal, coat of arms or watermark (AI or otherwise).
3. Text below the minimum size for the medium (section 4.3) or contrast below 4.5:1 (3:1 large text).
4. No clear focal point or reading order.
5. Essential content outside the safe area or cut by the platform crop.
6. Meaning carried by colour alone.
7. Rights not cleared (images, fonts, music, people’s likeness).
8. Off-brand palette, type or logo misuse.
9. Missing required disclosure or regulatory approval (paid partnership, AI realism, ARCON).
10. Inconsistent layout within a carousel or multi-page piece.

### 20.2 Scoring rubric (0–10 each; minimum 8 to pass)

| Category | 10/10 looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | The one message is understood in 5 seconds |
| Hierarchy | Clear first, second, third read; survives the blur test |
| Composition | Grid-based, balanced, deliberate whitespace, no tangencies |
| Typography | One or two families, systematic scale, correct sizes, good line breaks |
| Colour | Controlled palette, 60/30/10-style dominance, functional accent |
| Contrast and accessibility | WCAG AA met; colour-blind safe; alt text and captions ready |
| Imagery | Relevant, sharp, consistent treatment, purposeful crop, locally accurate |
| Content accuracy | Every word, figure and term correct and sourced |
| Brand | Recognisably on-brand with the logo covered |
| Series consistency and variety | Matches its set; varies from the previous post |
| Platform fit | Correct size, safe zones, survives the feed at phone size |
| CTA | The next action is obvious and singular |
| Professional finish | No artefacts, misalignments, widows, typos or export damage |
| Compliance and ethics | Rights cleared, disclosures in place, no deceptive patterns |

### 20.3 Universal pre-flight (every asset)

- The objective fits in one sentence, and the design delivers it.
- One unmistakable focal point; reading order is obvious.
- All text proofread letter by letter on the export (section 8.6).
- Type sizes at or above the medium minimums; line lengths and leading comfortable.
- Contrast checked with a tool; grayscale test passed.
- Everything aligned to a grid; margins and safe areas respected.
- Related items grouped; unrelated items separated.
- Palette and fonts match the brand; logo used correctly (or absent by design).
- Images sharp at 100%, cropped with purpose, rights cleared.
- Icons from one family; no decoration without function.
- No tangencies, near-misses, orphans or widows.
- Checked at actual size, at thumbnail size and on a phone.

### 20.4 Extra checks by format

**Social single image**

- Correct canvas (1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080) and 64–80 px margins.
- Headline 60 px+, body 32 px+, footnote 24 px+ at 1080 px width.
- Instagram 3:4 grid crop does not cut content; LinkedIn preview checked.
- Alt text written; post copy uses the same terms and numbers as the image.

**Carousel or document**

- Slide 1 works alone as a hook; last slide carries the CTA.
- Identical layout, type sizes, margins and label style on every slide; page indicators consistent.
- PDF has one page size throughout and is under 100 MB.

**Video and motion**

- Hook in the first 2 seconds; cover frame designed.
- Captions burned in, proofread, inside the 900 × 1400 px safe area.
- Text holds at least 0.3 s per word + 1 s; no more than three flashes per second.
- Licensed music; loudness about –14 LUFS.

**Print**

- 3 mm bleed, 3–5 mm safe zone, crop marks.
- 300 ppi images at final size; CMYK with the printer’s profile; ink limit respected.
- Small text in 100% K; fonts embedded; PDF/X-4.
- Hard proof and written sign-off for colour-critical or high-volume jobs.

**Web and UI**

- All component states designed; targets 44–48 px on touch.
- Body text 16 px+; responsive at 360, 768, 1280 px.
- Images compressed (WebP/AVIF); no layout shift.
- Keyboard focus visible; no deceptive patterns.

**AI-generated or AI-edited**

- Section 17.6 verification protocol complete and recorded.
- No phantom marks, invented facts or identity changes.
- Disclosure decided and Content Credentials kept.

### 20.5 Final sign-off

Approved by (name), on (date), for (platform and placement), with ARCON approval number if an advert. Store the approved file in the APPROVED folder (section 19.3).
