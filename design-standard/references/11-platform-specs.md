# 11. Platform and format specifications

Design for the destination: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) is the strongest default for feed posts on Instagram and LinkedIn, 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) for Reels, TikTok, Stories, Shorts and WhatsApp Status, and every vertical video must keep text inside the platform’s safe zone. Platforms change these often; re-check every quarter.

### 11.1 Feed and post sizes (checked November 2026)

| Platform and format | Size (px) | Ratio | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram feed, portrait | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | Default; profile grid now previews at 3:4 and crops top and bottom slightly ([Inro, Mar 2026](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-post-size)) |
| Instagram feed, tall portrait | 1080 × 1440 | 3:4 | Matches the profile grid preview |
| Instagram square | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | Centred, symmetrical designs |
| Instagram carousel | 1080 × 1350 every slide | 4:5 | All slides identical size |
| LinkedIn image, portrait | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | Takes the most space in the mobile feed ([Contentin, 2026](https://contentin.io/blog/linkedin-carousel-size/)) |
| LinkedIn image, square | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | Safe alternative |
| LinkedIn document (carousel) | 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080 per page | 4:5 or 1:1 | PDF preferred; max 100 MB, 300 pages, one page size throughout |
| Reels, TikTok, Stories, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | See safe zones below |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 × 720 | 16:9 | Readable at 160 px wide |
| Presentation slide | 1920 × 1080 | 16:9 | 4:3 only if the venue requires it |
| Email body | 600–640 wide | — | Live text, not one big image |
| Website hero | 1920–2560 wide | varies | Art-direct crops for mobile |

Facebook and X sizes change frequently; check the platform’s current help pages or the scheduling tool’s preview before final export.

### 11.2 Safe zones on 1080 × 1920 vertical video

Platform buttons, captions and usernames cover parts of the frame. Values below are blocked margins in pixels ([Adaptlypost, updated Oct 2026](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/social-media-safe-zones-2026-complete-guide)); interfaces change several times a year. **\[Standard, unofficial\]**

| Platform | Top | Bottom | Left | Right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok (organic) | 108 | 320 | 60 | 120 |
| TikTok (ads) | 108 | 370 | 60 | 120 |
| Instagram Reels | 210 | 310 | — | 84 |
| Instagram Stories | 100–150 | 200–250 | — | — |
| YouTube Shorts | 120 | 300–360 | — | 96 |

**Universal rule (MUST):** keep all essential text, faces, logos and CTAs inside a centred **900 × 1400 px** area on a 1080 × 1920 canvas.

### 11.3 Feed post safe areas

- Instagram 4:5 posts: keep critical content out of the top and bottom \~45 px so the 3:4 grid crop does not cut it.
- LinkedIn and Instagram images: 60–80 px margin on every side at 1080 px width; extra room at the bottom of carousel pages for the page counter.
- Profile photos are shown as circles: keep the face within the central 70%.

### 11.4 Adapt, don’t shrink

- When moving between ratios, re-compose: move the headline, re-crop the photo, re-flow text. Scaling a 1:1 design into 9:16 with bars above and below is a failure.
- Keep a master layout per ratio (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9) in the template set.
- Preview every final asset in the real platform (or a scheduler preview) before approval.

### 11.5 Text size on social, in practice

For a 1080 px wide graphic: headline 60–140 px, body 32 px or more, footnote at least 24 px, no more than about 30–40 words per slide. Below these, text becomes hard to read on phones (section 4.3).
